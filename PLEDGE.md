# Farside Research Open Source Pledge

Version 1.0. Effective October 27, 2026.

Farside Research LLC, a West Virginia limited liability company ("Farside"), makes these promises to everyone who receives StateFrame or any other project Farside releases under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 ("AGPL"). Farside intends every such recipient to be able to rely on and enforce them. They bind Farside, any entity Farside controls that maintains a project, and anyone who acquires Farside's copyright in a project or takes over its maintenance; Farside will make written acceptance of this pledge a condition of any such transfer.

1. **Released means released.** Every version Farside publishes under the AGPL stays available under the AGPL. Farside will not withdraw that grant from anyone who complies with the AGPL.

2. **No license switch later.** Farside will not release a later version of a project, or of any work derived from it under any name, under a source-available or other license that is not approved by the Open Source Initiative in place of the AGPL.

3. **The whole engine.** Every version Farside distributes to anyone, including commercial licensees, is published to the public under the AGPL, with its complete corresponding source in the public project repository, no later than Farside makes it available to anyone else. A commercial license covers the same code on different terms, and no order or contract with a customer overrides this. Separate application or integration code that Farside writes for a customer and the customer owns is not a version of a project; a modification of a project itself is, whoever owns or commissioned it. Two exceptions: a fix for a security vulnerability may be shared with affected parties under embargo for up to 30 days before it is published, and unreleased builds may be shared with Farside's own employees, contractors and testers.

4. **Contributions stay in the open.** When Farside distributes a version that contains a contribution, that version ships under the AGPL. The contributor license agreement lets Farside also offer a commercial license. Farside will never use it to offer a contribution only under commercial or proprietary terms.

5. **Fork without asking.** Anyone may fork a project and ship their own version under the AGPL, under their own name, as long as they comply with the AGPL and keep its required notices. That right comes from the AGPL. The project names and logos remain Farside's trademarks.

6. **Open even if we stop.** If Farside decides to stop maintaining a project, the last release stays under the AGPL and anyone can carry it forward. Farside will announce the decision in the project repository and on its website within 30 days of making it.

This pledge adds promises from Farside. It does not change the AGPL, the commercial license agreement or the contributor license agreement, and it does not add obligations for anyone who uses, contributes to or licenses a project.
